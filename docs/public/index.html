disha6.6 makes public-interest intelligence answerable to evidence.

The project is built around one rule: a conclusion is not complete until its source, policy decision, uncertainty, and evidence chain can be inspected.

Method

disha6.6 treats intelligence as a civic responsibility. It begins with a bounded mission, records what evidence was used, applies policy before action, and keeps unresolved claims visible instead of hiding them inside confident prose.

01

Source before signal

A claim has no product value unless its source, custody, update path, and verification state are visible.

02

Policy before action

Analysis routes through governed lenses so the system can distinguish public facts, controlled material, sensitive claims, and prohibited actions.

03

Evidence before output

The product records what was asked, what was examined, what was rejected, and what remains unresolved.

Why disha6.6 is different

disha6.6 is not positioned as a faster chatbot or a decorative command center. Its claim is narrower and more serious: public-interest reasoning should be auditable, policy-gated, and accountable to the citizen.

Constitutional lens

Public authority, citizen visibility, and institutional duty are first-class design concerns.

Deny by default

Controlled data and unsafe actions remain blocked unless a lawful authority path is present.

Provenance first

Every mission result must show the evidence chain or mark the gap for review.

Human restraint

Model output is advisory. Policy, evidence, and accountable review remain authoritative.

Architecture

disha6.6 is organized as a private intelligence runtime with a public doctrine surface. The public page explains the system; the private repo and runtime hold the implementation, credentials, data connectors, and operational workflows.

Mission

Receives the question, objective, scope, and allowed operating boundary.

Lens

Routes work through civic, cyber, finance, geospatial, and governance views.

Ledger

Preserves source, reasoning, policy result, evidence, and residual risk.

Runtime

Runs private product services, dashboards, agent tooling, and source connectors.

Safe read-only demo

The public demo should show capability without exposing private code, credentials, operational data, or unverifiable claims. Its purpose is to demonstrate the method, not to publish intelligence as fact.
