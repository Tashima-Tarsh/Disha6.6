## 3 Simple Steps to Start:

### Step 1: Install the "Continue" Extension in VS Code
1. Open VS Code.
2. Press **`Ctrl + Shift + X`** (open Extensions view).
3. Search for: **`Continue`** (by *Continue* - with 1M+ downloads).
4. Click **Install**.

---

### Step 2: Open Continue Panel
- Press **`Ctrl + L`** or click the Continue icon on the left sidebar in VS Code.
- The chat window will open.

---

### Step 3: Choose Your Model
In the dropdown at the bottom of the Continue chat, you will see your models already pre-configured:
- **`Qwen 2.5 Coder 7B`** (Recommended for coding & refactoring)
- **`Moonshot Kimi K2.5`**
- **`DeepSeek R1 8B`** (For deep step-by-step reasoning)
- **`Qwen 2.5 3B`** (Fast lightweight queries)
