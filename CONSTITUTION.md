# DISHA CONSTITUTION

**Supreme Governing Document of the Constitutional Evidence Operating System**

Version: 1.0  
Status: Binding for all future development  
Date: 24 October 2026

---

## Preamble

Disha exists to produce intelligence that can be inspected, challenged, traced to sources, policy-checked, and reviewed by a human.  
No model, agent, data feed, or automated process shall become invisible authority.

This Constitution is the highest authority in the system. All code, documentation, and operational practice must conform to it.

---

## Article I — Core Principle

**No claim becomes knowledge until it is:**
1. Sourced
2. Policy-checked
3. Recorded as immutable evidence
4. Human-reviewable

---

## Article II — Verification States (Mandatory)

Every piece of intelligence must carry exactly one of the following states:

| State | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| OBSERVED | Directly obtained from a source |
| INFERRED | Derived by reasoning from observations |
| CORRELATED | Linked across multiple sources |
| PREDICTED | Forward-looking statement by a model or heuristic |
| VERIFY_REQUIRED | Needs human or multi-source confirmation |
| VERIFIED | Elevated by evidence + policy + review |
| REJECTED | Explicitly disproven or disallowed |

Only `VERIFIED` findings may appear in authoritative reports without explicit caveat.

---

## Article III — Separation of Powers

1. **Models and Agents** are strictly advisory. They may propose, never conclude.
2. **Adapters** may only emit observations and EvidenceEvents. They may not write final findings.
3. **Policy Gateway** evaluates every high-impact action before execution.
4. **Human Review** is the final authority for elevation to VERIFIED.

---

## Article IV — Evidence Integrity

- The Evidence Ledger is append-only and hash-chained.
- Every policy decision, approval, rejection, and significant observation must produce an EvidenceEvent.
- Hash chain verification is mandatory for high-assurance paths.

---

## Article V — Trust Boundaries

- All external interaction occurs exclusively through the governed Adapter Bus.
- Authentication fails closed.
- Path containment uses proper relative resolution (`is_relative_to` or equivalent).
- High-risk actions default to proposal-only.
- No optional authentication that defaults to open.

---

## Article VI — Data & Legal Discipline

- Only lawfully obtained, properly authorized data may be ingested.
- Every adapter must declare legal basis and data sensitivity.
- Sensitive identity data requires explicit purpose limitation, minimization, and retention controls.
- Leaked, stolen, or credential material is permanently prohibited.

---

## Article VII — Uniqueness Mandate

Disha shall not compete on volume of collectors or speed of answers.  
Disha shall compete on **reconstructibility, accountability, and constitutional constraint**.

The permanent positioning is:

> “The only intelligence platform where every conclusion is reconstructible, policy-checked, and human-reviewable by design.”

---

## Article VIII — Amendment

This Constitution may be amended only by explicit, versioned, and reviewed change. Silent drift is forbidden.

---

**End of Constitution**
